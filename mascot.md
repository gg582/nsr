# Case File 01: Mr. Shroom

This suspicious mushroom emits a strange odor from his Gat-hat...
If you are invited to his home, half of the rice and side dishes he serves will be rotten and green.

---

A burnt smell always lingers in the third stall of his IT company's restroom.
He was once spotted in the hills on a weekend, wearing latex gloves.

---

Do not pry too deeply into his true identity.
He frequently searches for "Data Erasure Specialist" on Telegram. 
On the night his friend vanished, grilled mushrooms were served at the company dinner.
